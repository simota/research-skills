# Traps — route

- **"Quick question" is a description of the asker's patience, not of the evidence.** Classify by
  cost of error; confirm when the two disagree.
- **The full chain is not the rigorous choice by default.** Running five phases on a documented
  fact spends budget that a genuinely contested sub-question needed.
- **Skipping scope is the most expensive shortcut.** Retrieval with no stopping rule outspends
  every other failure mode, and produces a corpus nobody can bound.
- **Loops feel productive and often are not.** A second gap-fill that returns the same sources
  means the evidence does not exist. Report it rather than looping again.
- **Blocking questions surfaced at the end waste the whole chain.** Escalate the moment a phase
  raises one.
- **Aggregation silently drops gaps.** Assembling artifacts tends to keep the answers and lose the
  unknowns. Check that every phase's `gaps` survived into the deliverable.
