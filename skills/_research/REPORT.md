# Report Surface

Binding on every `research-*` skill, within its ownership. A brief, corpus, appraisal and findings
answer are different results. A shared surface never authorises a phase to produce the others.

## Record and view

The handoff (`_research/HANDOFF.md`) holds the actual phase artifacts, IDs, checks and gaps. The
surface is a view over those records, not the handoff repeated field by field. Missing upstream
artifacts are not evidence of zero gaps, and a filename alone is not the requested answer.

## The moments a run speaks

| Moment | What it owes |
|---|---|
| Start | The requested work and depth, when relevant, in one line |
| A question | The blocking decision and default; do not ask for a settled fact's invented prior |
| Mid-run | A changed plan, contradiction, blocked path or budget shortfall that changes the reader's decision |
| End | The phase-appropriate result below, its applicable check and unresolved work |

The tool call is already visible. Explain what changed, not merely that a call happened.

## At the end

1. **The requested result first.** Scope: brief. Source: corpus and access limitations. Appraise:
   assessment with advisory ceilings. None assigns a confidence label. For a findings answer,
   preserve a label only when synthesis assigned it; a ceiling is not an assignment.
2. **The evidence or phase check.** Use the checks actually performed. Claim support and reach
   counts apply to findings, not to a brief or ungraded corpus. Do not fabricate downstream counts.
3. **What remains unresolved.** `BLOCKED` and `UNSUPPORTED` stay visible; carry the other residuals
   in the handoff and surface them when they affect a decision. No unknown is erased by shortening.
4. **What is next**, only when there is a next action.

## Terminal ownership

On `standard`, `research-synthesize` supplies the supported answer itself: the primary answer and
sub-question answers, source IDs, assigned confidence, necessary conditions, contradictions and
unknowns. It may be inline or in its synthesis artifact, but the user receives the answer rather
than only a ledger or a path. This is a complete deliverable without an extra reporting phase.
`research-report` is for audience-shaped writing; it changes neither claims nor their strength.
`research-route` forwards the terminal answer, without reassignment or gap loss.

On `quick` and `vet`, no synthesis ran. Report a source-attributed, bounded answer and say
"no synthesis pass; confidence unassigned". Do not turn an appraisal ceiling into a label. If the
request needs cross-source reconciliation or a new inference, return that need for synthesis;
an evidence gap is not solved by writing an unlabelled but confident conclusion.

## Size and strength

Use the shortest result that meets the request (`_research/SIZING.md`). Link extended evidence
when present; never require a separate write-up merely because the tier is `standard`.
Shortening may change wording, but not semantic strength: preserve modality, causality,
population, version, as-of date and attribution next to the assertion they qualify. A summary
must be safe to read alone. "May", "associated with", "in this sample", "as of DATE", "the company
states" and "evidence is mixed" do not become certainty, causation, generality, currency,
unattributed fact or proof. Confidence labels never override the source's actual qualification.

The requested scope is the deliverable. Neighbouring concerns and future possibilities stay out.
A real safety or correctness problem is explained at whatever length it needs: cut noise, not risk.
When the reader asks for more, answer from the records at the length that question deserves.
