# Traps — source

- **Search-result snippets are not sources.** They are optimised extracts and routinely invert the
  document's actual claim. Open it or drop it.
- **Five articles, one press release.** Syndicated tech and business coverage collapses to one
  origin constantly. Check publication dates within 48h of each other and near-identical numbers.
- **Recency ranking hides the primary.** Indexes surface the most recent commentary; the specification,
  paper, or filing that everyone is discussing sits several pages down. Query for it by name.
- **One vendor page is two kinds of source.** It is a primary record of what the product is
  documented to do, and marketing about how it compares. Retrieve it, tag both uses, and let
  appraisal tier each use separately.
- **Absence of results has two causes.** Nobody published it, or you asked in the wrong vocabulary.
  Rotate vocabulary before concluding the former — the field's insiders rarely use the outsider term.
- **Forward chaining beats keyword search for recency.** Who cites this / depends on this / links
  here finds the newest relevant work that has not yet accumulated search authority.
- **Undated pages should be treated as old.** Content management systems drop dates; a page with no
  date is not recent evidence, whatever its position in the results.
